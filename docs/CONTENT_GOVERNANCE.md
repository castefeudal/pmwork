# Content governance

## Evidence order

Official standards → official framework guides → professional bodies → international standards → primary research → reputable practitioner research → mature practice → opinion.

Every substantive recommendation is treated as one of: normative standard, official guidance, accepted practice, contextual recommendation, or heuristic. Method-fit scoring is explicitly labeled a heuristic.

## Update policy

Framework pages contain a version and reviewed date. Recheck official owners at least every six months and whenever a new edition is announced. Update the registry first, then content claims, tests, completeness matrix, and changelog.

## Terminology and copyright

Use professional Russian with the English original where translation may be ambiguous. Never copy long passages from paid guides. Keep quotes exceptional and short. Do not use owner logos or claims of certification/affiliation.
